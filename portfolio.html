About Me

Hi! My name is Rhyanna and I'm a 3rd year Product Design major with a minor in User Experience and Interaction Design at Drexel University.

I believe products should provide a transformative experience.

Through play, user-centered research, and iterative prototyping, I want to examine what makes an experience with a product forgettable and change that to a memorable interaction that inspires critical thought and feeling.

I aim to create designs that help inspire magic in everyday moments, surprise in monotonous routines, and curiosity in dull instances.
